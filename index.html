Meet the Meows

Mr. Meow

The wanderer. Restless paws, wide eyes, always chasing the next horizon.

Mrs. Meow

The dreamer. Gentle heart, quiet eyes, finds beauty in everything.

He was a wanderer who never stayed. She had a quiet garden she never left. One afternoon he walked past her fence, and she opened the gate. They walked out together, and everywhere they went, the world grew beneath their paws.

His backpack holds every treasure from a thousand miles of wandering. Her flower is the garden she chose to carry rather than stay behind. Together, their love became a world.

The World

Welcome to Purrnia

Purrnia is a world that grew from the love of two small cats who chose each other and walked forward together. Every stone, every stream, every warm gust of wind is an echo of that first morning they stepped through the gate.

"The world purrs. It has always purred. If you are very still and very kind, you can feel it in your paws."

Eight regions, each with its own gentle magic, its own creatures, and its own way of being warm.

🔥

Emberwood Thicket

Hearth-Crafting

An ancient forest of golden light, empathetic fires, and homes carved into colossal trees. Tea-Cup Foxes sleep on warm pastries.

🌊

Lullaby Lochs

Intuitive Wayfinding

Terraced lakes under eternal twilight. The water purrs when touched. Bubble-Frogs comfort the lonely.

🌻

Sun-Draped Terraces

Generative Cultivation

Mediterranean hills of perpetual harvest. Plants grow when complimented. Silk-Weaver Bees leave handmade gifts.

🪁

Chiming Canyons

Epistemic Unveiling

Rose-quartz mesas and crystal geodes. The wind carries you. Kite-Rays swim through the air.

❄️

Velvet Tundra

Empathetic Transmutation

Diamond snow that never chills. Auroras drip warm light into healing pools. Woolly Moth-Cats are living blankets.

📜

Glimmering Archives

Nostalgic Anchoring

A city of petrified books and floating lanterns. Open a book to feel a stranger's happiest memory.

☁️

Cloud-Spun Canopy

Catenary Weaving

Walkable clouds sculpted by hand. Aura-Sheep change color with the sky. Rainbow bridges connect islands.

🧵

Bramble-Patch Bazaars

Harmonic Synthesis

Underground markets lit by glowing berries. Broken things mend with gold. Patchwork Badgers find what you've lost.
